# Aidemia Lesson Plan — 30s Plush Ad

Статус: production direction approved after three creative debate rounds.

## Audience and promise

- US K–12 classroom teachers.
- Product proof: Aidemia Lesson Plan turns teacher inputs into a structured,
  editable draft.
- Teacher remains in control: the draft is reviewed, adapted and made classroom-
  specific by the teacher.
- `30 seconds` is the duration of this ad, not a product-speed claim.

## Chosen concept

**TEACHERS → INPUT → DRAFT → EDIT**

The plush alphabet is the attention system and the infographic is the proof
system. The letters begin as individual physical objects, assemble into
`TEACHERS`, then become three large tactile state labels. We show a single
readable product flow instead of a wall of tiny UI chips.

## 30-second spine

| Time | Visual and plush action | Product proof | On-screen copy | VO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Individual plush letters fall, collide, squish and magnetically assemble. | Brand hook only. | `TEACHERS` | “Teachers.” |
| 3–6s | The word settles; a soft blue line draws toward the product card. | Position Aidemia as a teacher tool. | `AI FOR TEACHERS` / `Planning your next lesson?` | “Planning your next lesson?” |
| 6–10s | Plush `INPUT` lands beside a large clean form. | Topic, subject, grade and duration are entered. | `INPUT` / `Science · Ecosystems · Grade 5 · 45 min` | “Start with your topic, subject, grade and time.” |
| 10–16s | Plush `DRAFT` assembles; the card opens in three readable sections. | Structured draft: warm-up, time blocks, materials. | `DRAFT` / `Warm-up` / `Time blocks` / `Materials` | “Get a clear, structured lesson plan draft.” |
| 16–21s | One section gets a tactile highlight; a cursor changes a line. | The draft is editable. | `EDIT` / `Make it yours.` | “Then edit it to fit your classroom.” |
| 21–25s | `INPUT`, `DRAFT`, `EDIT` align as a simple horizontal system; letters breathe and settle. | Workflow is understandable at a glance. | `YOUR IDEA → YOUR DRAFT` | “Your idea stays yours.” |
| 25–27s | Plush `TEACHERS` returns around the card; no new claim. | Teacher agency and brand memory. | `YOU STAY IN CONTROL.` | “You stay in control.” |
| 27–30s | Calm final frame, logo and blue CTA anchor. | Product/category identification. | `Aidemia` / `AI for Teachers` / `Try Aidemia today.` | “Try Aidemia today.” |

## Visual rules

- Use the existing pile, seams, bevels, side volume and soft-body deformation.
- Keep one dominant action per beat; impacts are readable, not frantic.
- Use at most three visible infographic states at once: `INPUT`, `DRAFT`,
  `EDIT`.
- Keep the UI large enough for a 9:16 mobile placement. No microscopic fields or
  paragraphs.
- Use Calm Classroom accents: white/soft sky base, near-black copy, brand blue
  connectors, marker yellow for a small input highlight, coral only for one CTA
  emphasis.
- Do not use rainbow backgrounds, robots, replacement-teacher language, fake
  student records, exact savings, user counts or perfect-output claims.
